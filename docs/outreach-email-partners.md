## Two things that make or break this
1. **It must be a *followed* link.** Ask their webmaster to confirm it isn't tagged
   `rel="nofollow"` / `sponsored` — only followed links move Authority Score.
2. **In-content, not a footer/sidebar logo.** A link inside a relevant paragraph is
   worth far more than a logo in a partner strip.

## After they say yes

  treatment page is stronger).
- Record it in `backlink-targets.md`: date, partner, URL, anchor, followed? (y/n).
