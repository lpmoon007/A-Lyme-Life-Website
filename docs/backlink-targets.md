# A Lyme Life — Backlink & Outreach Target List

**Why this exists:** The site is now technically healthy (crawlable, fast, clean
schema), but GSC shows pages ranking on page 4–5 (desktop avg position ~49).
The gap is **domain authority** — authoritative sites linking to alymelife.com.
This is the single highest-leverage growth lever left, and it's off-site work.

**How to use this:** Work top-down. Tier 1 targets already link to *competing*
Lyme content (Semrush surfaced them as your rivals' backlink sources), so they're
the most likely to link to you too. Log every attempt in the tracking table at
the bottom. Aim for **2–4 quality links a month** — steady beats spammy.

**Golden rule:** Never buy links, use link farms/PBNs, or mass-submit to junk
directories. Google penalizes it, and you already have a disavow file
(`docs/disavow-alymelife.txt`) cleaning up prior bad links. Every link here is
earned through genuine relevance or Christina's real story.

## The outreach method (keep it simple)

For each target: find the right contact (resource-page owner, blog editor,
podcast host), send a short, specific, genuine pitch. Template:

> Subject: Patient-perspective piece for [their site/show]
>
> Hi [name] — I'm Christina Carter of A Lyme Life. My family lived a decade of
> chronic Lyme; my daughter was patient #1 at a new immune-retraining program in
> Mexico, and my husband was patient #1 for a post-Lyme T-cell immune reset.
> I noticed your [specific post/episode] on [topic] — I'd love to offer
> [a patient-side companion piece / to share our story on the show / a resource
> your readers would find useful]. Here's the relevant page: [specific URL].
> No pressure either way — thank you for the work you do for this community.

**Rules that keep it effective:** one specific ask, reference their actual
content, link to the most *relevant* page (not always the homepage), lead with
the story, keep it short.

## Realistic expectations

- **2–4 quality links/month** is a healthy, safe pace for a site this age.
- Authority compounds slowly: expect ranking movement on competitive terms over
  **months, not weeks**. The foundational links (Tier 6) + one podcast can start
  moving the needle first.
- Watch the **Referring Domains** count in Semrush and the buried money pages'
  positions in GSC (chronic-lyme-treatment, supplements, find-a-doctor) — those
  are the ones authority will lift off page 5.
